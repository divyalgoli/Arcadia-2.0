How it works

No two boards are the same, but every one plays by the same three rules.

01

Pick a shape

Start with a bold arrow silhouette, spell out a word, or paint your own outline freehand. That shape becomes the board.

02

A puzzle fills it in

Long connected arrow pieces tile the entire shape, packed edge to edge. Every layout is generated to guarantee at least one valid clearing order — never a dead end.

03

Clear it, tile by tile

Tap a piece to send it off the board. It only escapes if nothing else sits between it and the edge. Guess wrong and you lose a life — three strikes and it's a reshuffle.
