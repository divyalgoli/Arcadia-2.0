Pong

Fast, local two-player table tennis. Grab a friend, share the keyboard (or the screen), and rally to 11.

Game Instructions

🖥️ Desktop controls

Player 1 W up · S down Left paddle
Player 2 ↑ up · ↓ down Right paddle
Anytime Space pause / resume

📱 Touch controls

On tablet or mobile, each player drags a finger up and down on their own half of the table — left half moves the left paddle, right half moves the right paddle.

🏓 Rules of play

  • The ball serves automatically toward a random side after every point.
  • Hit the ball back with your paddle before it passes you — miss it and the other player scores.
  • Where the ball strikes your paddle changes its angle: hit with the edge to add spin.
  • First player to reach 11 points wins the match.
