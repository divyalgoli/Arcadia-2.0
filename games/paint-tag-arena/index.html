How to Play

RULES

Objective

One player is the Chaser, one is the Runner. Touch the Runner to tag them — roles swap and the Chaser scores a point. If the timer runs out first, the Runner scores instead. First to 5 points wins the match.

Player 1 Controls

Move: W A S D • Dash: Left Shift

Player 2 (Opponent) Controls

Move: I J K L • Dash: U — not Shift, that key belongs to Player 1.

Power-Ups

Speed Boost temporarily raises your max speed. Shield blocks a tag while active. Double Dash grants an extra dash charge. Freeze Trap freezes whichever player doesn't collect it.
